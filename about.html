About GBHS Pilot Dadu

Nearly three decades of building character and confidence

Our story, our purpose, and the people who lead our school community.

Our History

Serving the boys of Dadu for over three decades

Government Boys High School, P.H. Pilot, Dadu was established under the Sindh Education Department to give boys across Dadu access to a disciplined, quality education close to home. The school began with a small campus and a handful of teachers.

Today, GBHS Pilot Dadu has a dedicated science laboratory and computer lab, and has grown into one of the most established government schools in the district — without losing the community feel it started with.

What Guides Us

Mission, vision, and values

Our Mission

To help every student become a confident, disciplined, and capable young man — ready for higher secondary education, for work, and for life beyond both.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a school where academic excellence and genuine character development happen side by side, not as a trade-off.

Our Values

Curiosity, integrity, respect, resilience, and community — principles we teach explicitly, not just assume students will absorb.

Headmaster's Message

A note from Mr. Ghulam Hussain Jatoi

"When parents visit GBHS Pilot Dadu for the first time, I ask them to do one thing: watch how our students talk to each other in the hallways. That, more than any exam result, tells you what kind of school this is.

We hold high academic standards — our results reflect that. But we also spend real time on the things that don't show up on a report card: how to disagree respectfully, how to recover from failure, how to lead a team. My door is open to any parent who wants to see that in action.

Thank you for considering GBHS Pilot Dadu for your child's education. I hope to welcome you to our campus soon."

Mr. Ghulam Hussain Jatoi
Headmaster, Government Boys High School, P.H. Pilot, Dadu
