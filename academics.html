Academics

A curriculum built for every stage of growth

From Grade 1 foundations to Matric board exams, each level is designed around how students actually develop.

Grades 1–5

Primary

Core literacy, numeracy, science, and arts, taught by homeroom teachers who know each child well.

Grades 6–8

Middle School

Subject-specialist teaching begins, with expanded science labs, technology, and elective choices.

Grades 9–10

Secondary

Board examination preparation across science, arts, and commerce tracks.

Curriculum

How we teach, assess, and grade

Curriculum Overview

Our curriculum blends the national board syllabus with supplementary material in critical thinking, digital literacy, and communication skills, reviewed and updated every academic year.

Assessment & Grading

Students are assessed through a mix of continuous classwork, term exams, and project-based work. Report cards are issued each term and are available through the parent portal.

Departments

Subject departments and what they cover

Mathematics

From arithmetic foundations to calculus, with a dedicated Olympiad prep track.

Science

Physics, chemistry, and biology with weekly hands-on lab sessions from Grade 6.

English

Language and literature, with a strong emphasis on writing and public speaking.

Computer Studies

Basic computer literacy and IT fundamentals from Middle School onward.

Social Studies

History, geography, and civics, with an emphasis on current events and critical thinking.

Languages

Urdu and Arabic language instruction alongside English, with additional foreign language electives.

Arts

Visual arts, music, and drama, treated as core subjects rather than electives.

Physical Education

Daily PE plus competitive teams in football, cricket, and athletics.

Islamiyat & Pakistan Studies

Compulsory subjects taught across Middle and Secondary school.
