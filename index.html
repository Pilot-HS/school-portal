About GBHS Pilot Dadu

Rooted in tradition, built for the future

Established under the Sindh Education Department, Government Boys High School, P.H. Pilot, Dadu has served the community of Dadu for over three decades. We follow the official Sindh Textbook Board curriculum from Grade 1 through Matric.

Our mission is simple: give every boy in our community access to a disciplined, quality education — regardless of his family's income.

Academic Programs

A clear path from Grade 1 to Matric

Grades 1–5

Primary

Foundational literacy and numeracy in Urdu, Sindhi, and English, alongside science and physical education.

Grades 6–8

Middle School

Subject-specialist teaching begins, following the Sindh Textbook Board curriculum.

Grades 9–10

Secondary (Matric)

Board examination preparation in Science and General groups, under BISE Sindh.
