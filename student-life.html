Student Life

School is more than what happens in class

Clubs, sports, arts, and leadership opportunities that help students find their people and their strengths.

Clubs & Societies

Debate, Qirat & Naat, scouting, and a science club — student groups that meet weekly after class.

Sports Teams

Competitive football, cricket, and athletics, with inter-house and inter-school fixtures.

Scouting & Co-curricular

Pakistan Boy Scouts Association troop, plus calligraphy and speech competitions each term.

Debate & Public Speaking

Weekly practice and a full competitive calendar, including national tournaments.

Community Service

Term-based service projects connecting students with local organizations and causes.

Educational Trips

Grade-level trips within Dadu and Sindh, including an annual Grade 10 study visit.

Student Council

Elected representatives from each grade meet monthly with school leadership.

Competitions

Regular participation in district-level science fairs, debate, and sports tournaments.

House System

Four houses, one community

Every student is sorted into one of four houses — named Iqbal, Jinnah, Liaquat, and Fatima — on their first day at GBHS Pilot Dadu. Houses compete throughout the year in sports, academics, and discipline, building cross-grade friendships and school spirit.

The annual House Cup is awarded at our end-of-year assembly, and house points are visible to students and parents through the portal.
